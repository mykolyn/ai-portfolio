Process

  1. 01

    Intro call

    30 minutes. Your goals, constraints, and whether we’re the right fit. If we’re not, we’ll say so and point you somewhere better.

  2. 02

    Proposal

    Within a week: scope, milestones, team, fixed price or capped time-and-materials. No surprises.

  3. 03

    Build, in the open

    Shared Slack channel, weekly demos, a living eval dashboard. You see progress, not status reports.

  4. 04

    Launch & handover

    Production rollout, docs, a recorded walkthrough and 30 days of support. Your team owns it.

FAQ

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Yes — about half our work is with seed to Series A teams. A Discovery Sprint is usually the right first step when the idea is still forming.

Which models and providers do you use?

Whatever fits the job. We’re fluent with frontier APIs and open-weight models, and we’ll benchmark options against your own data before recommending one.

Who owns the code and IP?

You do, in full, from the first commit. We work in your repositories whenever possible.

How do you handle sensitive data?

NDAs as standard, least-privilege access, no training on your data, and we’re happy to work inside your cloud or VPN. We can align with HIPAA and SOC 2 controls.

Can you join our existing team rather than build alone?

Absolutely. Many engagements are embedded: we pair with your engineers so the know-how stays when we leave.
